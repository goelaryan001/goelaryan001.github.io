About

I'm a Master's student in Computer Science (Data Science specialization) at USC, by way of a Bachelor's in Computer & Communication Engineering from Manipal University Jaipur, and research stints at ISRO and IIT Roorkee before that.

What actually holds my interest is the moment a dataset stops making sense at face value — a support ticket that seems to raise churn until you check who bought it, a query that runs fine until the table crosses a few million rows. I like tracing those down to the real mechanism, then building the pipeline, model, or system that acts on it correctly.

Right now that spans classical ML and statistics, database internals, and increasingly agentic GenAI systems — I'm deliberately building breadth across data science, data engineering, and ML engineering rather than narrowing early.
