About

I'm a Master's student in Computer Science (Data Science specialization) at USC, by way of a Bachelor's in Computer & Communication Engineering from Manipal University Jaipur, and research stints at ISRO and IIT Roorkee before that.

What actually holds my interest is the moment a dataset stops making sense at face value — a support ticket that seems to raise churn until you check who bought it, a query that runs fine until the table crosses a few million rows. I like tracing those down to the real mechanism, then building the pipeline, model, or system that acts on it correctly.

Right now that spans classical ML and statistics, database internals, and increasingly agentic GenAI systems — I'm deliberately building breadth across data science, data engineering, and ML engineering rather than narrowing early.

DEDSMLE

ChainSignal: Crypto Market & On-Chain Anomaly Detection

A batch pipeline for crypto market and on-chain anomaly detection: 1,000 coins × 365 days from CoinGecko plus 1.64M ERC-20 transfers from Etherscan, loaded into ClickHouse and processed with PySpark, Dagster and dbt. Compares a robust z-score, Isolation Forest and LOF using 887 planted anomalies and a backtest on documented events (Isolation Forest recovered 43% of 12σ combination anomalies vs 11% for the z-score; the Kelp DAO hack was missed and reported). Flags Celer Network's $1.5B volume on an $18M market cap as a wash-trading candidate, not a verdict.

DSDADE

Telecom Churn Paradox: Causal Inference & Uplift Modeling

A leakage-safe churn pipeline on synthetic data with a built-in Simpson's paradox: the support add-on looked 6.5pp worse on raw churn but lowered churn in every risk quartile once stratified. Nested CV, isotonic calibration, a cost-optimal threshold, SHAP and segmentation, S/T/X-learner uplift models and a simulated A/B test, then re-run on real IBM Telco data (ROC-AUC 0.840), where the paradox did not replicate and is reported as such.

Python · scikit-learn · XGBoost · SHAP · DuckDB
